# WinIsland Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

WinIsland is committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming community for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, appearance, ability, nationality, religion, or
other personal characteristics.

## Expected conduct

- Be kind, patient, and respectful.
- Discuss ideas and trade-offs without attacking the people proposing them.
- Give clear, constructive feedback and remain open to correction.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information without permission.
- Help make project spaces welcoming to newcomers and established contributors alike.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation,
trolling, spam, sustained disruption, and other behavior that makes participation unsafe or
unwelcome are not permitted. This applies to public and private interactions connected to the
project.

## Scope

This code applies in WinIsland repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, community
groups, and other official project spaces. It also applies when someone is representing WinIsland
in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact a WinIsland maintainer privately. If
you do not already have a private contact channel, open a short issue asking a maintainer to contact
you and do not include sensitive details in the public issue.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Maintainers will consider the
context, protect reporters from retaliation, and avoid conflicts of interest when reviewing a
report.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit content and may issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban someone from project spaces. The response will be proportional
to the behavior, its impact, and whether it is repeated. Maintainers are expected to follow a
higher standard and may not take part in reviewing reports in which they have a conflict of
interest.

Questions or appeals should be raised privately with a maintainer who was not involved in the
original decision whenever possible.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Rust Code of Conduct](https://rust-lang.org/policies/code-of-conduct/)
and adapted for the WinIsland community and its moderation process.
